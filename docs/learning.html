Featured Videos

Because dek hockey relies on quick hands, ball control, body position, and agility on foot, off-ice and ball-specific training videos can be useful practice references for players at home.

Youth Drills

The Daily Warm-Up

Video: 5 Minute DAILY Stickhandling Routine
Channel: How To Hockey - Coach Jeremy

Skill focus: Quick hands, wrist flexibility, and top-hand control.

Equipment needed: Dek hockey stick, orange ball or green biscuit, smooth flat surface such as a driveway or garage.

The drill: Five minutes every day before picking up your phone. Focus on looking up, not down at the ball.

Advanced/Adult

Deep-Dive Workouts

Video: 30-Minute Stickhandling Session (Follow Along!)
Channel: Hockey Training

Skill focus: Endurance, reaction time, and wide-reach puck handling.

Equipment needed: Training ball, two cones or shoes spaced about 3 feet apart.

The drill: Follow along for a longer workout. Great for pre-season conditioning or weekend homework. Split the effort into 40-second work intervals and 20-second rest.

All Levels

Team Tactics & Dek Strategy

Video: Stick Skillz Ball Hockey (Stick Handling)
Channel: StickSkillz

Skill focus: Body positioning, shielding the ball from defenders, and shooting mechanics on foot.

Equipment needed: Net or target tarp.

The tip: Notice how players set their feet and transfer weight without relying on skate glides. Practice pulling the ball slightly toward your feet before releasing the shot to change the angle on the goalie.
